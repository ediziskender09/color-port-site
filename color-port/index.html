How to play

Three rules, then it gets clever.

  1. Tap a free containerOnly a container with nothing on top of it can be lifted.
  2. Match the shipIt boards the ship of its own color. Any other color waits in the waiting area.
  3. Keep room to waitFill every ship before the waiting area is full, and the harbor is cleared.
