How to play

Three rules, then it gets clever.

  1. Tap a free containerOnly a container with nothing on top of it can be lifted.
  2. Match the shipIt boards the ship of its own color. Any other color waits in the waiting area.
  3. Keep room to waitFill every ship before the waiting area is full, and the harbor is cleared.

Meet the crew

Five characters keep the harbor running.

They greet you, explain what is new, and celebrate with you when the last ship sails.

Captain Otto

Harbor captain

Knows every quay by heart. He teaches you the ropes and cheers each cleared harbor.

Mia

Crane engineer

Shows up whenever something new arrives: a booster, a block of ice, a bigger ship.

Bruno

Dock worker

The strongest hands on the dock and the softest heart. He announces the Hard levels.

Pip

Seagull

Sees everything from above and cannot keep it to himself. The small hints are his.

Rocco

Stowaway

A raccoon who keeps mixing up the cargo. Mystery containers and padlocks are his doing.

New twists as you go

The harbor keeps surprising you.
