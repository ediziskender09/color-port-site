About us

A small studio with clear rules.

Glint Games is an independent studio making puzzle games for phones. Our first game, Color Port, is in testing on iPhone and Android. These are the three things we hold ourselves to.

01

Clear on the first tap

You should understand a Glint game within seconds. The depth comes later, one new idea at a time.

02

Fair by design

Every Color Port level is solved by our own solver before it reaches you. If you are stuck, there is a way out.

03

Your data stays yours

No ads, no account, and we never sell data. What Color Port measures to fix crashes and keep levels fair is written in plain words on its privacy page.
